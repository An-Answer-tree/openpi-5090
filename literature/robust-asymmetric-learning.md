# Robust Asymmetric Learning

Source: [Robust Asymmetric Learning in POMDPs](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/warrington21a.html)

A privileged teacher can be a poor target for a partially observing student
when the teacher's action depends on information unavailable to the student.
For ACPD, teacher strength alone is therefore insufficient; the transferred
target must also be predictable from the weak view.
