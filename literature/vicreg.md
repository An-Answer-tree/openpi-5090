# VICReg

Source: [Variance-Invariance-Covariance Regularization for Self-Supervised Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04906)

VICReg combines invariance with separate variance and covariance terms. For
ACPD, this means the current variance floor addresses constant-coordinate
collapse but does not prevent redundant cue coordinates or ensure that the cue
contains task-relevant privileged information.
